# Tamga Wallet

**Show what's needed. Keep the rest.**

An EU-compatible (EUDI) digital identity wallet and the first wallet listed on [Tamga Network](https://tamga.network).
Your credentials stay on your phone; you share only what is asked — for example a yes/no proof that you are over 18,
without your date of birth.

- **Website:** [tamgawallet.com](https://tamgawallet.com)
- **Status:** Android test build; iOS and app stores coming soon
